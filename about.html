Will It Come Back?

About

Will It Come Back? turns any picture into noise with one simple rule, and then brings it back after an exact number of steps. It began with a viewer comment on our YouTube video about zipping a smiley: “make a website where we can do the same thing to an image.” This is that website.

What makes it different

  • The exact step a picture comes back, computed from its pixel cycles, even when the number has hundreds of digits.
  • Four classic rules (perfect shuffle, Arnold's cat map, baker's map, ring twist) plus a rearrange mode that builds a new picture from the same pixels.
  • Video export in YouTube 16:9 and Shorts 9:16, made on your device.
  • Private by design: your pictures never leave your device.

Who we are

Computed makes videos and tools about the surprising math inside everyday things. Watch on YouTube and follow on X.

概要

Will It Come Back? は、1 つの規則で絵を砂嵐にし、決まった手数で元に戻す無料のツールです。YouTube「Computed」の動画への「同じことを画像でできるサイトを作って」というコメントから生まれました。戻る手数を画素の輪から正確に計算し、YouTube 横動画（16:9）・ショート（9:16）の動画を端末内で書き出せます。画像は端末の外に出ません。
