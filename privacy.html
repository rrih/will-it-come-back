Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Your pictures

All image processing happens in your browser. Pictures you open, scramble, rearrange or record are never uploaded to our servers or anyone else's. Videos and PNGs you save are created on your device.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used (for example which rules and sizes people try). Google Analytics uses cookies and collects data such as pages visited, device type and approximate location. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings or at www.aboutads.info. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Local storage

The site caches its own files in your browser so it works offline. Pictures you add are kept in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) on your device only, so they are there next time; delete them from the Images panel or by clearing site data. Your language, theme and export format are remembered in local storage.

Contact

Questions: reach us on X (@computedch) or through the Computed YouTube channel.
